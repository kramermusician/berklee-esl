to or for? with or from? in, on, or at? Blast the right word.

How to play

A sentence appears with a missing word. Words fall from space. Shoot the one that fits. Let the wrong ones fall away.

Laptop: ← → move, space or ↑ fire. P pause, M mute.

Phone or tablet: tap a word to fly under it and fire. Drag to move. Tap empty space to fire straight up.

A wrong shot costs a heart and shows you the right answer. Letting the right word escape costs a heart too. Speed and streaks score big.

Power-ups (shoot them or fly through them): Shield: blocks one mistake Slow-mo Hint: one wrong word explodes 2x points

Lander bonus: gravity! ↑ or hold the screen to thrust, ← → or drag to steer. Set down gently on the right pad.

Boss: a long text with many blanks. Hit the right word for each one. The boss shoots back. Dodge.
