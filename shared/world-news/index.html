News in Your Language

Public radio newscasts from around the world, updated every day. Pick a button and listen.

How to use this

Pick your language and listen to today's news. Most of these are made for people who already speak the language, so they run at full speed. Because full speed is the point, you will miss things the first time through. Listen once without stopping, then listen again and write down five words you caught. Bring those five words to class.

The green buttons are the exception. Those broadcasts are built for learners, so the announcer slows down and a transcript comes with the audio.

Several of these stations are run by their national governments. Listen for how the same story changes from one country to the next.
